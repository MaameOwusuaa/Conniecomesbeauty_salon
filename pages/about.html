Who We Are

Conniecomes Beauty Salon is a professional beauty destination dedicated to helping clients look and feel their best through quality beauty services and premium hair products.

We specialize in professional wig installations, custom wigs, wig revamping, frontal installations, hair treatments, manicure, pedicure and makeup services.

Our goal is to provide every client with a beautiful, comfortable and personalized experience.
